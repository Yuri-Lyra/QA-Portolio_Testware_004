# Risk Register (Testware #004)

Score = Probability x Impact (1 to 5 each). Level: **High** from 15, **Medium** from 8, otherwise **Low**. Test priority follows the score, highest first (risk-based testing, CTFL 5.2).

| ID | Category | Risk | P | I | Score | Level | Mitigation / control action | Status | Owner |
